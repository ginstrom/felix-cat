3.3.7 Manage Your Glossaries

You can have any number of glossaries open at one time—the only limit is the physical memory and disk space available on your computer. Use the Glossary Manager dialog to manage your various glossaries.

The process for managing glossaries is identical to that for managing memories; see 3.2.12 Manage Your Memories for details.
